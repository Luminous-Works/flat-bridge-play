# Waters of Flat Bridge

A Jamaican folklore adventure set in the 1780s, at Flat Bridge on the Rio Cobre. You are Samuel, a stagecoach driver born on the Montague estate, who stumbles on River Mumma's gold. From the gorge to Bog Walk, Spanish Town, the King's Highway and Kingston harbour, you are pursued by Redcoats, the Rolling Calf and River Mumma herself.

**Play:** https://luminous-works.github.io/flat-bridge-play/

**Meta Quest (hands, no controller needed):** open the link in the Quest Browser with hand tracking on and press **Enter XR**. The world appears as a diorama on the table in front of you.
- Left pinch and pull: walk (pull further to run). Quick left pinch: jump.
- Right pinch: talk and act; hold it at the helm.
- Point at a reply and pinch to choose it.
- Wrist buttons (above the left wrist, poke with your right fingertip): Bag, Rum, Ale, Fruit; Dodge and the counters in the Rolling Calf chase.

**Computer:** W A S D or the arrows to move, Shift to run, Space to jump, E to talk or act, 1 2 3 for replies, I for the knapsack, H for help and the Music and Ambience sliders. Headphones help.

Written, built and scored by Tony Cunningham. Theme: "Yuh Cyah Ketch Mi" by Tonecii. Built with the Immersive Web SDK and Three.js.

This repository holds the built game only.
