# Horse sound credits

Clips in this folder were cut (trimmed, faded, peak-normalised to -3 dBFS, mono Ogg Vorbis) from recordings on
Wikimedia Commons, downloaded 2026-11-13. Played by `src/song.ts` (`loadHorseClips`, `horseClip`).

with your own recordings or confirm the source.
